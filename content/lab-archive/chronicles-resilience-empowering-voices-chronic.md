Two things worth passing on this week.

Dr. Leah Houston wrote on KevinMD about an elderly patient who couldn't find a ride home after discharge. It's a small story about a large gap. Healthcare systems treat "out the door" as the finish line, when getting home safely is part of the treatment. [Read it here](https://www.kevinmd.com/2022/07/theres-no-one-to-drive-your-patient-home.html).

The same gap shows up at medical conferences. The people living the thing being discussed are rarely the ones discussing it. Put a patient on the agenda, not just in the audience, and the conversation changes.
