I recently taught another cohort of Austria's Global Incubator Network program, focused on the skills entrepreneurs rarely get taught directly: inspiration, storytelling, and resiliency.

Resiliency isn't a personality trait some people have and others don't. It's a toolkit, built deliberately, that you reach for when a setback arrives, a job loss, a health crisis, a failed launch, and it determines how fast you get back to functioning rather than whether you eventually do.

Building one comes down to a few concrete things: knowing your actual strengths well enough to lean on them under pressure, having a real coping mechanism that isn't avoidance, a support system you'll actually call, the ability to be unsentimental about your own mistakes, and enough orientation toward what's next that you don't get stuck re-litigating what already happened.

None of that is complicated. Almost none of it is instinctive either, which is exactly why it has to be built on purpose, before the setback arrives, not improvised during it.
