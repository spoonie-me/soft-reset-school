Of living experience.

Of managing on low resources under conditions of uncertainty.

Of navigating complex systems while running on substantially less energy than healthy people ever have to account for.

Of our own illnesses.

Of advocating.

Of building communities and bridges.

Of falling and getting up, over and over again.

Of hope.

We are healthcare.

Pay us as experts.

Treat us as experts.

Put us in decision-making positions.

This was written after a lot of frustration being a patient keynote speaker.

When you invite a patient to speak, consult, or participate, it isn't charity. It's an honor to have that expertise in the room, an obligation to seek it out, and the right thing to do. Don't build a pity slot for the patient speaker. Embrace what we actually know.
