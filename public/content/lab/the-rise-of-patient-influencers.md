Patients with a platform are starting to do more than share their story. They're shaping drug development, patient journey design, and medical education, because they're the only people in the conversation who've actually lived the thing being designed.

I have some standing to say this: it's what I've built a career doing. The part nobody's solved yet is compensation and ethics. A patient's insight gets treated as free content when a consultant's identical insight gets invoiced. That gap is the real story here, not the platform growth.

The organizations that figure out how to pay patient expertise like the expertise it is will get better input than the ones still treating it as a testimonial.
