# Soft Reset School — Vision

*Draft for Roi, 2026-10-25. Every page, price and lesson should be checkable against this.*

## Who it is for

Anyone going back to work at a pace they can sustain. That includes people returning after
physical illness, mental-health difficulties, burnout, caring for someone, or a career break.
The school does not ask about a diagnosis and does not define people by one. The front door
never uses illness words ("bed-bound", "crash", "spoonie", diagnosis lists). Roi's own story
stays on the About page because it is true and it is his.

Spooniversity remains the home of the chronic-illness community and keeps the spoonie identity.

## What it teaches

**Human skills in the AI age.** Work that AI makes more valuable, not less: judgment,
explaining, teaching, presenting, designing for real people, and directing AI tools well.

Structure:
1. **Core: Human Skills in the AI Age** (about 25 hours). Shared by everyone. See `docs/CORE_TRACK_OUTLINE.md`.
2. **Specialisations**, each its own credential at about 100 hours:
   - AI-Assisted Software Development
   - AI Automation Consulting
   - AI-Augmented Medical Coding
   - Digital Accessibility QA
   - next: UX Design (pairs with Accessibility QA), then Teaching and Training

## Principles

1. **Capacity, not diagnosis.** Design for variable energy and concentration. Every module has a lower-energy path and nothing is timed.
2. **No promises.** No job, income or outcome guarantees. A certificate shows a passed exam.
3. **Pay once.** No subscription for learners. A free first lesson in every track. Hardship codes by email, no proof asked.
4. **Never performance-only.** Anything involving speaking or presenting has a written or recorded route.
5. **Not therapy.** Say so plainly, and link to crisis support where relevant.
6. **Honest depth.** A credential is sold only when its exam bank is verified and it meets the content-depth bar.

## Decisions (2026-10-25)

1. **Structure:** a shared core plus separate specialisation credentials. The core is not a separate purchase: it is included with any specialisation, and its first module is free for everyone, like the one-lesson trial.
2. **Organisations:** seats at €99 per track, once, delivered as single-use codes by email (the same mechanism as hardship codes). Ongoing partnerships (a pool of seats topped up through the year plus a progress summary) are priced on request. The For Teams page now promises only what exists: no admin dashboard, CSV onboarding or custom paths until they are built.
3. **Archived foundation tracks:** kept, hidden and not sold. Removing about 1,000 lessons is hard to reverse and nothing depends on it. They are raw material for the core. Revisit if deploy time or upkeep becomes a problem.
4. **Spooniversity:** link, never copy, and only to courses that remain listed there: Sick Bootcamp (starting out), Advocacy in Action (asking for adjustments), Carer Resilience (carers returning). Data Literacy and Health Data are unlisted on Spooniversity, so the core owns its own evidence-judging module.
